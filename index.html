About

I am a PhD candidate in Economics at the University of Technology Sydney (UTS), where I was awarded an International Research Scholarship. I submitted my thesis, Climate, Energy, and Uncertainty in Macroeconomic Dynamics: Essays in Applied Time Series Econometrics, on 6 October 2026, and it is now under examination. Before my PhD, I completed an Honours degree in Economics with First Class Honours at UTS (2021) and a Bachelor of Commerce, majoring in Economics and Finance, at the University of Sydney (2019).

My research focuses on climate and energy economics, applied macroeconomics, and the analysis of economic and financial uncertainty. My methods include structural vector autoregressions, vector error correction models, local projections, Bayesian state space models and dynamic factor models.

From June 2024 to October 2026, I worked as a teaching tutor at UTS, teaching Intermediate Macroeconomics and Business Statistics. My teaching interests also include microeconomics and econometrics. From 2022 to 2026, I served as President of the Vietnamese Students’ Association in Australia (SVAU) and Deputy Chair of the Vietnam Australia Innovation Network (NIC AU).
